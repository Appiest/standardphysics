RESEARCH CONCLUSIONS YOU MUST NOT REVERSE WITHOUT NEW EVIDENCE

- Genuine Gaussian splatting already runs here: Brush v0.3.0 on Metal/WebGPU,
  compressed SPZ, rendered by Spark. Existing Brush initialization already uses
  calibrated ARKit cameras and LiDAR seed points. "Add LiDAR seeds" alone is not
  a new method and does not explain prior failures.
- Four scans were aligned. Photographic overlap residuals around 4–5 cm are not
  independent absolute measurement accuracy. The bottom shared patch was small;
  whole-room drift remains possible. Do not casually redo transforms or overwrite
  them to conceal rendering seams.
- We preserved about 3,524 photos plus videos, poses, RoomPlan and aggregate LiDAR
  meshes. No separate raw IMU or per-frame depth/confidence was found previously.
  Mesh-rendered depth is derived supervision. Do not invent raw sensor inputs.
- Existing HQ center trial: 1920px, up to 3M Gaussians, SH2, 30k steps, roughly an
  hour. 34-view background PSNR was ~18.68 at 15k/20k and ~18.58 at 30k. It retained
  blur/ghost people and was rejected as a replacement. These are historical,
  confounded comparisons, not the matched baseline for your new experiment.
- The 34 evaluation images are captured views. Separate 1.15 m seated renders
  have no established matching ground-truth photographs. Never compute seated
  PSNR/SSIM against another renderer and call it real-world ground truth.
- Full-room RealityKit jobs failed imageAlignment; a partial center mesh had
  sharper detail and large holes. Failure does not prove all photos unusable.
- DeepFusion and FusionViT are object detectors, not pretrained Gaussian builders.
  Fresh random neural heads are not a trained reconstruction. Inspect current
  experimental splats/ code before trusting names like train or deepfusion.
- Structured-Li-GS supplies useful surface anchors, normals, bounded count and
  depth/normal/flattening ideas. Its RTX A5000 experiments do not establish Mac
  support, whole-room runtime or a universal quality win. Fewer Gaussians do not
  automatically mean less end-to-end compute.
- LVI-GS uses CUDA on RTX4070Ti; its ~3s result is for a keyframe, not a whole floor.
  LiGSM uses isotropic Gaussians/view-independent RGB and RTX4090 experiments.
  Li-GS is a distinct paper whose full methodology was not verified. SplatAD uses
  custom CUDA and a learned appearance decoder, not a drop-in SPZ pipeline.
- msplat (https://github.com/rayanht/msplat) is a real Apache-2.0 native Metal
  trainer. Upstream ~70s refers to 7k iterations; a 30k garden run reports 700s,
  3.51M Gaussians on M4 Max. No verified 2–4 GiB training footprint here. Compile
  and benchmark before making local performance claims. Its iOS fork is not proof
  of fast full-floor phone training.
- OpenSplat supports Metal but AGPL integration requires deployment-specific
  license review. SuGaR is not simply MIT licensed. Do not import restricted code
  into the product without checking its exact license and dependencies.
- MVSplat/pixelSplat are view-synthesis models, but CUDA operators, trained weights,
  licensing, scene scale and domain shift require verification. Porting them is
  outside this night's critical path.

Covariance:
- Keep centers fixed on the measured support initially.
- Build stable tangent/normal frames, including parallel and antiparallel cases.
- Covariance = R diag(s_t1^2,s_t2^2,s_n^2) R^T.

NUMERICAL EVALUATION POLICY: HARD GATES FIRST, SCORE SECOND

The companion JSON is authoritative for initial numeric values. All thresholds
below are provisional product experiment rules, not perceptual laws or accuracy
certificates. Freeze them before tuning. Never relax them after seeing failure.

Required metrics from paired actual captured views:
- P: mean per-view static-region PSNR in dB, with full-frame PSNR also reported.
- Fix metric RGB to decoded sRGB float64 in [0,1], channel-averaged masked MSE;
  PSNR=10*log10(1/max(MSE,1e-10)). Record perfect matches separately; the 100 dB
  cap makes identical-image fixtures finite and applies equally to all runs.
- U: mean static-region SSIM, fixed implementation/kernel/data range. Compute SSIM
  on full images and average only windows entirely within the fixed valid mask;
  do not zero masked pixels and introduce artificial agreement at mask borders.
  Use Gaussian weights, 11x11 window, sigma=1.5, data_range=1, population covariance,
  channel-averaged RGB; pin library/version and identical settings for all renders.
- R: minimum across preregistered critical ROIs of candidate-minus-baseline PSNR.
  Each ROI needs valid source pixels. No silent deletion of difficult ROIs.
- H: fraction of fixed known-photo-supported evaluation pixels not covered by
  appearance, from a common alpha-capable renderer; coverage threshold alpha>=0.5.
  Freeze backgrounds/threshold and inspect false filled surfaces visually. If a
  comparable coverage pass cannot be produced, this gate is unknown, not passed.
- T: median cold end-to-end wall time, including necessary preprocessing, decode,
  masks, pose adaptation, build/train, export/compression. Report stage times.
- M: peak process-tree footprint with documented GPU/shared-memory scope.
- Z: actual SPZ bytes. F: p95 rendered frame time under the common viewer protocol.

Use cold-cache-equivalent runs (new derived run caches, not deleting user/global
caches) and separate warm runs. OS disk cache is not under complete control; label
that limitation. Three runs for baseline and every candidate receiving an eligible
score, alternating execution order. Smoke trials may collect one run and raw
validation quality to reject weak ideas cheaply, but their aggregate score stays
null/provisional until repeat and review requirements pass. Reduce the number of
scored candidates to stay within the night budget; do not omit repetitions.
Report each value, median, range and training randomness. No cherry-picked fastest
run. For M use the maximum observed comparable peak; Z use the maximum across
repeated outputs; F use the worst of per-session p95s. Record GPU synchronization.
Do not label vm_stat system counters as application peak memory.

Viewer protocol: same viewport/device/browser/renderer revision, all assets loaded,
10-second warmup then 60-second fixed path, 3 sessions, no competing GPU jobs.
Use actual rendered-frame/GPU timing where available; requestAnimationFrame rate
alone is not rendered FPS. If proper frame timing unavailable, record missing F
and make score ineligible rather than inventing it. Record browser footprint too.
Phone timings require the physical device; desktop viewport emulation is separate.

LPIPS is diagnostic only unless its implementation/weights are already available
and fixed for both sides. No invented LPIPS, no automatic reweighting when absent.
Report per-view results, worst views and a paired bootstrap uncertainty diagnostic
by independent trajectory group (1000 resamples, seed 1729); do not claim statistical
significance with too few groups or highly correlated frames.

Hard gates (all must pass for an eligible candidate):
G1 input/split/code/evaluator hashes consistent; artifacts valid; original captures
   unchanged. Implementation code may change between explicitly versioned trials;
   no unrecorded code/evaluator changes after a trial configuration is locked.
G2 correct calibration/axes/color/export and required meaningful tests pass.
G3 every expected view/ROI rendered at the fixed resolution; no missing/invalid metrics.
G4 mean PSNR drop <=0.20 dB, mean SSIM drop <=0.005, every critical-ROI PSNR drop
   <=0.30 dB, and worst per-view PSNR drop <=1.0 dB relative to matched baseline.
   Mean full-frame PSNR drop must also be <=0.50 dB. This conservative gate can
   reject useful person removal when test photos contain people; report that
   tradeoff as exploratory instead of changing masks/thresholds after the fact.
G5 uncovered supported pixels increase by <=1.0 percentage point; no disappeared
   critical feature, new misleading filled surface, or new/worse person ghost in
   mandatory paired visual inspection. Unknown visual review means unknown gate.
G6 footprint below resource cap; valid end-to-end timings and same-device render
   measurements; p95 frame time no more than 10% slower than baseline.
G7 display change preserves measured collision/export/revision/auth boundaries;
   no unsupported claim of unseen detail, physical accuracy or ADA compliance.
   Preserve the authoritative measured mesh/graph and fallback surface inventory,
   verified by hashes and invariance tests. H is appearance coverage, not independent
   geometry validation. Pruning unsupported appearance is allowed only with the
   loss/support change disclosed; it does not establish retained physical accuracy.

No aggregate score can compensate for a failed/unknown gate. A missing value is
null with an error, never zero, perfect, or omitted from the denominator.

Score eligible validation candidates only (baseline = 50):
clip(x)=min(1,max(0,x)). B denotes matched baseline; C denotes candidate.
qP = clip(0.5 + (P_C-P_B)/2.0)
qU = clip(0.5 + (U_C-U_B)/0.04)
qR = clip(0.5 + R/2.0)
Q = 100*(0.40*qP + 0.30*qU + 0.30*qR)
gain(b,c)=100*clip(0.5 + log2(b/c)/2.0), for positive finite values only.
S = 0.60*Q + 0.20*gain(T_B,T_C) + 0.08*gain(M_B,M_C)
    + 0.07*gain(Z_B,Z_C) + 0.05*gain(F_B,F_C)

Example: identical outputs/resources score 50. A twofold speedup alone adds 10
points, giving 60 if all other components are unchanged. It still cannot pass if
an outlet disappears, coverage fails, or memory/timing evidence is missing.

Eligibility is not selection. A validation finalist additionally needs S>=55 and
one explicit tradeoff lane:
QUALITY: PSNR improves >=0.50 dB AND SSIM improves >=0.010; T_C<=1.25*T_B;
         M_C<=1.10*M_B; Z_C<=1.25*Z_B; all hard gates pass.
EFFICIENCY: T_B/T_C>=1.5; M_C<=M_B; Z_C<=Z_B; all hard gates pass.
Confirm timing improvement exceeds observed run-to-run noise; if ranges overlap
substantially, label the claim inconclusive even if the median ratio passes.
The lane and score are separate requirements deliberately: meeting the quality
lane at its maximum cost limits can still score below 55 and remain unselected.
Keep a Pareto table for useful candidates not satisfying these provisional lanes;
do not call them production replacements. A fast visibly inferior result is at
most a labeled draft candidate, not photorealistic completion.

For masked averages improving while full-frame quality worsens, inspect why and
retain both. Freeze manual review labels before revealing method names if feasible.
Visual review includes actual exported SPZ in the product viewer, not only trainer
screenshots. Review statuses must identify human, independent agent, or self-review.
No “human approved” status without a human. Unknown review cannot be self-filled.
Labeled agent self-review is allowed; a human is not required for experimental
eligibility. Missing usable frame timing intentionally prevents an eligible score,
but does not block implementation, exploratory renders or the final honest report.
