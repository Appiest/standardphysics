CONTROL MEASUREMENTS — OPTIONAL FOR CAPTURE, REQUIRED FOR STRONG DIMENSION CLAIMS
Moffett currently has no independent control measurements supplied by the user.
LiDAR-derived measures are useful estimates; do not invent tape comparisons.
Ask for controls only when needed and explain exactly which conclusion they unlock.
For each measurement record endpoints in photo, value/units, instrument/method,
instrument resolution/known limits, actor/time and relationship to scanned surface.
Target at least 10 distinct controls per site: multiple clear door/route widths,
counter section heights/extents and outlet heights as available. Reserve at least
five not used in calibration. Include every claimed measurement type and near-limit
cases. If site lacks a category, mark that claim unsupported or not applicable
with evidence; don't substitute ten readings of one easy wall as full validation.
Field-measure the actual scenario: clear opening, usable counter section, real
obstacle clearance. A box's overall dimensions may not be the legal measurement.
Repeat critical measurements to expose endpoint/instrument error. Do not round
away a near-threshold discrepancy. Need more precision? ask for targeted evidence.
Door force, hardware operation, floor properties, electrical function, signage
and other non-geometric conditions require their own suitable observations/tests
and source-defined procedures; they do not appear magically from a LiDAR mesh.
