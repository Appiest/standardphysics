STANDARD PHYSICS: DISTANCE FROM THE SHOP-SCANNING VISION
Audit date: 2026-09-21
Code examined: 3fe4fba and the current working tree, including uncommitted work.
Scope: capture -> evidence -> scene -> useful objects -> accessibility screening
-> redesign -> web/phone experience/export. Read-only source/product-data audit;
no product code changed, new paid recognition calls, deployment or capture run.

ASSESSMENT
There is a substantial working prototype: authenticated capture/upload, measured
scene graphs, web inspection, selected geometric rule checks, furniture-layout
search, revision history and exports. There is not yet a demonstrated repeatable
journey in which a new shop is scanned and its outlets, TVs, cashier and restroom
are reliably identified and turned into trustworthy, complete ADA redesign advice.

The closest useful product is an ASSISTED accessibility screening tool: an owner
confirms roles and photos, verifies uncertain dimensions, and receives selected
route/counter/furniture suggestions. That is materially closer than autonomous
whole-shop assessment. Neither should be described as finished or certified.
Rendering is one gap; evidence ingestion, semantic integration, measurement
validation and legal applicability are at least as important to this vision.

Do not report a percentage complete from test counts or lines of code. Feature
implementation, real-scan accuracy, legal scope and field acceptance are different
dimensions. The evidence supports a prototype-stage judgment, not a release date.

CAPABILITY SCORECARD
1. Scan and upload a room — IMPLEMENTED; CURRENT PHYSICAL JOURNEY UNVERIFIED.
   iPhone RoomPlan/ARKit records room geometry, calibrated RGB/poses, LiDAR and
   other artifacts; authentication, Keychain token storage and resumable upload
   exist. Four preserved Moffett captures contain 3,524 photo/pose pairs.
   Evidence: apps/ios/StandardPhysics/Capture/{FrameRecorder,PoseRecord,
   LidarMeshRecorder,SceneCaptureConfiguration}.swift; Upload/ScanUploadClient.swift.
   There is no current recorded new-shop phone acceptance spanning the whole flow.

2. Convert a new scan into a usable scene — BASIC PATH IMPLEMENTED; DETAILED
   PHOTOGRAPHIC PATH PARTLY EXPERIMENTAL.
   Worker ingest calls RoomPlan parsing, labeling and discovery; generic texture
   jobs and GLB viewing exist. Rooms can also display clean approximate geometry.
   The newest Moffett 500px bake/repair remains offline scripts rather than the
   API's new-scan pipeline. Current visual quality still fails the requested goal.

3. Automatically combine an entire shop — NOT DEMONSTRATED.
   Product combine is owner alignment/drag/rotation, not automatic registration.
   Moffett's overlap investigation and saved transforms were a special workflow.
   Multi-room artifacts do not establish an unattended new-shop capability.

4. Locate outlets — ALGORITHMS AND REVIEW/REACH UI IMPLEMENTED; REAL ACCEPTANCE OPEN.
   Photo detections, surface attachment, reconciliation, persistent review and
   estimated wheelchair approach/reach exist. They have regression fixtures.
   Current A-102 revision 4 has zero outlet/candidate nodes, zero attachments and
   zero photographic observations. A synthetic demo is not an actual detection.
   docs/progress/PROGRESS_MOFFETT_OUTLET_REPAIR.json still has real acceptance unfinished.

5. Locate televisions — BASIC CATEGORY SUPPORT; NO DEDICATED VERIFIED FLOW.
   RoomPlan/generic labeling can represent televisions; one other stored scan
   ('room 6') has a Television object. Moffett has none. Generic discovery merges
   tv with screen; there is no demonstrated evidence-backed TV-finding workflow
   or TV-specific accessibility decision. Recognition, screen visibility and
   reach to a control would be separate questions with different evidence.

6. Locate cashier/service counter — MANUAL ROLE CONFIRMATION WORKS.
   API/UI can mark an object as service counter, persist a revision and recheck.
   A-102's one service counter is explicitly owner-labeled. A measured box or
   model-produced name does not establish customer side, accessible section,
   register location, counter type or the applicable approach requirement.

7. Locate/access/check restroom — MAJOR MISSING PRODUCT CAPABILITY.
   No dedicated restroom recognition, room classification, fixture relationship
   or complete assessment flow was found. The rule engine asks for restroom
   evidence rather than measuring full toilet/lavatory/grab-bar/door requirements.
   Labeling a door 'restroom' alone would not establish that the room is accessible.

8. Check selected ADA-related geometry — IMPLEMENTED WITH MATERIAL LIMITATIONS.
   Rules and measurement code cover selected aisle/route, door, turning/passing,
   counter and related geometric conditions. Source sections and URLs are stored.
   Default assessment is Tier 1. Catalog = 18 rules; 16 ledger entries; two Tier-1
   rules lack verification; higher tiers are not enabled by default.
   Neither this catalog nor the default checks cover whole-shop compliance.

9. Suggest an actionable redesign — WORKS FOR A LIMITED CLASS OF FURNITURE MOVES.
   Search proposes translations/rotations of movable items, re-measures, rejects
   invalid/newly problematic layouts and saves revisions. There is synthetic
   proof of the find/move/recheck cycle; actual-scan findings often remain asks.
   Lowering/replacing a counter, moving plumbing, installing grab bars, changing
   a doorway or relocating electrical services are not solved by that search.

10. Explain/export the result — IMPLEMENTED, BUT EVIDENCE/PRECISION REMAIN LIMITED.
    Architecture ZIP, captured/measured/inferred distinctions and revision-pinned
    output exist. They do not prove unknown placement reasons, outlet function,
    exact centimetre accuracy, construction feasibility or ADA compliance.
    Outlet UI currently shows frame/confidence text rather than its actual crop.

PRIORITY FINDINGS
P1 — New-shop evidence can arrive AFTER processing starts.
apps/ios/StandardPhysics/Upload/UploadViewModel.swift:110-124 finalizes core data,
then uploads optional photos/video/manifest while polling status. Lines263-273
classify those artifacts as optional. stages.py:59-66 declines discovery inputs
when required detailed evidence is missing; lines134-146 retain RoomPlan nodes on
discovery failure. Later photo arrival can enable texturing, but the initial
ready graph is not proof that semantic discovery used complete photo evidence.
Required outcome: distinct geometry-ready/evidence-ready/discovery-complete states
and a verified, idempotent recognition job after the evidence receipt is complete.

P1 — Object recognition and correction are not fully connected or validated.
stages.py:112-124 calls labeling/discovery, not
discovery/semantic_corrections.py:256 apply_secondary_semantic_corrections.
Whiteboard attachment and photo-driven sofa/table correction tests therefore do
not prove production use. detect.py has generic object prompts/class handling;
surface_attach accepts image_url but discover.py does not supply it to the outlet
attachment call. OutletPanel.tsx:269-277 shows only frame/confidence text.
Required outcome: actual source crops, world location/support surface, calibrated
confidence/unknown state, persistent corrections and live object findings on a
previously unseen shop through the real ingest path.

The older recognition run claimed missing API keys. Root verified that both
DISCOVERY_API_KEY and OPENROUTER_API_KEY are present after the normal .env load.
Their values were not printed; no request was sent, so validity/service access
remain untested. Do not ask for a new login solely because the historical run
did not inherit configuration. That historical blocker is not current evidence.

P1 — Two reproducible regressions undermine measurement/check reliability.
services/api/tests/test_combine_scans.py:68 expects merged floors at Z=0 but gets
Z=-1.0; fails both full suite and isolated rerun. This establishes a contract
failure, not that every saved Moffett dimension is exactly one metre wrong.
packages/agents/tests/test_tiers.py:181 expects a deep protruding shelf to produce
a problem but receives passes. This is a Tier-2 test, not proof default Tier-1
production always runs that check. Repair both before expanding acceptance.

P1 — Legal verification is weaker than the product description implies.
All 16 rule-ledger entries name 'Automated threshold check (needs a person's
second check)' and second_check_by is null. rules/verification.py:59-60 enables
matching entries without requiring that human second check. Stored thresholds
and source text are useful, but are not evidence of professional review of all
conditions, exceptions, applicability, local amendments or measurement methods.
Counter checks chiefly implement a parallel-approach path, not every accessible
counter configuration. Route exceptions, passing/turning shapes, door approaches,
reach orientations and restroom fixtures have limited or absent implementations.
Required outcome: explicitly scoped rule versions, applicability questions and
reviewed rule tests against real examples; no global compliance claim.

P1 — Unanswered checks and measurement uncertainty do not fully reach the owner.
checks/observation.py:50-59 says Unevaluated never reaches owners;
services/api/standardphysics_api/stages.py:199-202 logs these gaps and returns the
assessment. Absent roles may produce no observations. Thus 'no findings' cannot
establish that every relevant item was checked. Six explicit questions do exist
for thresholds/hardware/force/floor/restroom/reach; that is a useful foundation.
Rules use numerical tolerances, not experimentally established capture confidence
intervals. Outlet UI can consume user-supplied uncertainty but that does not
create measured scan error bounds. No tape/control measurements for Moffett were
provided, so absolute centimetre accuracy has not been established.
Required outcome: visible pass/problem/needs-verification/not-applicable with
reasons, plus measurement provenance and threshold decisions aware of uncertainty.

P2 — Photographic quality remains below acceptance; reports overstate one gate.
Latest commits f8f5faa/3fe4fba improve offline mesh evaluation, GLB handling and
display-geometry drift. Current c7 captured views still show triangular gaps,
fragmented surfaces and people. Root visually inspected frame0086 and frame0095.
c7 frame0086 reports 86.62% photographed target pixels and a 10.19% largest hole.
The handoff/checkpoint says critical ROI>=98% passed, but actual values include
96.63%,95.98%,94.58%,93.66%; it did not pass that policy. Resolution uses a
sqrt-determinant proxy instead of the policy's weaker-axis/source/mip-aware check.
Do not use these summaries as release evidence. Four room bakes are not a verified
stitched walkthrough or actual browser/phone performance result.

P2 — Deployment and physical-product acceptance are incomplete.
The default Dockerfile installs neither Blender nor the optional reconstruction
extra; real scans can therefore fall back to boxes even where local scripts work.
worker.py:1-6 assumes one process per database; ordinary multi-worker scale-out is
not supported by that queue model. Physical iPhone upload-to-results, browser
runtime, detailed mesh loading and a new-store usability test remain unverified
in this audit. These are separate from passing component/unit tests.

WHAT THE CURRENT REDESIGN CAN AND CANNOT MEAN
Evidence-backed example: 'This modeled route narrows to X; the selected rule
requires Y for the confirmed scenario. Moving this movable table by Z improves
the modeled clearance, and these other modeled checks still pass.' The engine
has the basic mechanisms for that statement, subject to the limitations above.
It cannot yet support 'Every outlet is reachable, the restroom is compliant and
this shop is ADA compliant after these edits.' Outlet presence/function, public
restroom scope, door operation, floor slope/surface, fixture placement, precise
dimensions and local applicability need separate evidence and checks.

HOW FAR / NEXT PRODUCT MILESTONES
An assisted single-shop pilot is within the existing architecture: considerable
code can be reused. It still needs integrated recognition, trustworthy result
states, repaired regressions and physical validation. It is not one render fix
or another overnight model run away from dependable unattended operation.
The full multi-shop, multi-room vision also requires new restroom/fixture and
rule-applicability capabilities plus generalization evidence across real shops.
No defensible calendar estimate follows from this audit; measure it after the
first unseen-shop acceptance run and the source-quality/accuracy study.

Milestone A: one complete assisted journey, without a perfect 3D renderer.
Use a clear measured plan plus original photographs and markers. On a NEW small
shop, upload all evidence, review detected outlets/TV/counter/restroom entrance,
show their photo crops and approximate measured positions, confirm customer stops,
and return one supported selected-rule issue with a verified furniture proposal.
Do not require game-like graphics before learning whether that workflow is useful.

Milestone B: repeatable evidence and measurement quality.
Create manually checked inventories and independent physical dimensions across
several different shops; keep evaluation images/shops out of tuning. Report
precision/recall for objects that were actually photographed, unobserved areas,
position/height errors, near-threshold uncertainty, missed hazards and false
alarms. A missing detection must not be reported as proof no outlet/restroom exists.
One photo can show a useful candidate; multiple consistent views strengthen it.
Device/capture failures must produce an actionable targeted recapture request.

Milestone C: scoped professional accessibility screening and broader redesign.
Review applicable rules with a qualified accessibility practitioner, expose gaps,
support relevant counter/door/restroom/reach conditions, and separate furniture
moves from fixture/construction recommendations requiring further assessment.
Validate every recommendation against the measured revision and uncertainty;
retain evidence photos, exact rule scope, before/after measurements and remaining
unknowns. Add automatic multi-room alignment and improve mesh quality in parallel
once the core outcome works. Physical phone and deployed-server tests are required.

No new Gaussian-splatting work is necessary for these milestones. Existing splat,
SplatX and DeepFusion experiments do not establish any missing semantic or legal
capability. Keep them outside the critical path for this user-requested mesh product.
